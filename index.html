Team Role Division (Everyone has a task!)

Divide your 13 members into these 4 units. During preparation, each group works on their specific task.

🎙️ Opening Speakers (3 students)

Prepare and present the best, bulletproof 2 minutes to kick off the debate. Define the core argument of your side.

⚔️ Attackers (4 students)

You don't care about defense! Think of the most vicious and uncomfortable questions targeting the opposing team's weaknesses for the cross-examination phase.

🛡️ Defenders (3 students)

Anticipate the opponent's attacks. What will they ask? Prepare an argumentative defense so you can strike back immediately during the cross-examination.

📜 Closing Speakers (3 students)

Stay silent during the debate and take notes on what is being said. At the end, you have 1.5 minutes for a final emotional and rational summary.
